FAQ

Things people always ask.

Do I need to know CAD? +
Nope. The studio is built so anyone who can drag a window around can design a part. Start with a primitive, push faces around, cut holes with the boolean tool. There's no jargon and no menu burrowing.
How fast do orders ship? +
Right now, while I'm printing orders personally on a Bambu Lab A1 mini, expect 3–5 business days to print + 2–5 days ground shipping. Once we scale to a fulfillment partner, the target is 7 days door-to-door, anywhere in our 14-country shipping zone.
What can't I print? +
No weapons or weapon parts, no copyrighted characters, no medical devices, nothing illegal where you live or where it ships. Full list lives in our Acceptable Use Policy.
What if the print arrives broken? +
Email a photo within 14 days and we'll refund or reprint at our cost. Shipping damage and manufacturing defects are on us. Full terms in the Refund Policy.
Can I import an STL I already have? +
Yes — there's an Import button at the top of the studio that accepts both STL and OBJ. Import it, scale or rotate, then order it like anything else.
Do you train AI on my designs? +
No. Your designs are private to your account. We don't share them, sell them, or use them as training data. If you delete your account, your designs are wiped within 30 days.
